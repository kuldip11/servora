# A shared table

Design established after competitor and product research. Taste direction: **One motif**, seed `685276820`. The restaurant table becomes the circular visual centre of four operational views. Cobalt carries the principal product view; smaller circles identify workspaces. The live thing is an inspectable service tour and local workflow playground.

| Role              | Value     |
| ----------------- | --------- |
| Ground            | `#eef0f3` |
| Product/white     | `#ffffff` |
| Secondary surface | `#e2e5ea` |
| Ink               | `#0b0c0e` |
| Supporting ink    | `#5d6470` |
| Primary cobalt    | `#1f2de6` |
| Accent ink        | `#ffffff` |

Typography: self-hosted Roundo 400/500/600, with its SIL OFL included. One type redraw replaces the initially drawn Chillax because its closed-source redistribution conditions were not established for this public repository. Palette and composition are retained from the new draw. No forest/lime tile-board styling or existing Servora marketing copy is carried forward.

The grid has a 1320px maximum, equal fluid gutters and an 8px spacing rhythm. Marketing body copy is 17–19px; small labels are at least 13px. Main breakpoints are 1100/760px with small-phone adjustments. Buttons have one rounded shape, clear focus and 44px minimum target. Only open overlays receive shadows.

Navigation groups products by workspace and supporting management jobs. Small primitives support a wide kitchen page, narrow floor/guest views, a POS split and focused management guides. The application launcher is a utility. Forms preserve the existing lead payload and report real delivery success/failure; errors are shown after interaction.

Product figures use the actual imported application components with source-conforming sample fixtures. Captions identify component captures, not authenticated production screenshots. Their application styling is preserved: authentic source UI takes priority over a generic marketing/app style prescription. No invented hardware or dashboard metrics are used.

One contextual hospitality scene may support the story, explicitly identified as an illustration of service rather than a customer endorsement. The application screenshots remain the product evidence. Competitor screenshots are research references, never production assets.

Motion: one shutter entrance on the principal image, quiet control feedback and meaningful demo state changes. Reduced motion removes animation/smooth scrolling. No opacity-based text fades or expensive scroll effects.

Research principles: restaurant context from Toast/SpotOn; inspectable UI from Square/TouchBistro; operational grouping from Restroworks; direct task vocabulary from regional product sites. Exact competitor layouts, branding and claims are excluded. Actual renders must be reviewed against this system, including phone/tablet/desktop screenshots and working interactions.
